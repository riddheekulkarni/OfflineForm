# Volunteer Operating Manual
**Device ID:** Tab [ X ] *(Numbered 1 to 7)*  
**Purpose:** Attendee Registration & Data Recording

---

## 1. Starting Up
1. Unlock the assigned iPad.
2. Open the application by tapping the **Registration App Icon** located directly on the **Home Screen**.
3. Keep the iPad plugged into a power bank or wall charger throughout your shift.

---

## 2. Checking App Health
Before taking entries, glance at the **App Health** indicator on your screen:
* **Green / Healthy:** Everything is operating normally. Storage is working and ready to store records safely.
* **Warning / Red:** If the status displays any alert or storage issue, notify your lead volunteer immediately.
* **Important:** Never reset the iPad settings, clear website history, or delete app cache, as all attendee entries are stored safely inside the device memory.

---

## 3. Entering Attendee Information
For every attendee who approaches your desk:
1. Fill in the required fields:
   * **Full Name**
   * **WhatsApp Number** *(Check that the 10 digits are accurate)*
   * **City**
2. Tap the **Submit** button.
3. The screen will display a brief success message and reset for the next person.
4. Each completed submission will automatically increment the **Sync Pending** counter on the screen.

---

## 4. Sync Routine (Every 30–45 Minutes)
Do not wait until the end of the shift to sync all data. Follow this quick routine regularly:

1. Check your **Sync Pending** count.
2. Tap the **Sync All** button.
3. The iPad will immediately open the built-in **Mail app** with your data file attached.
4. **Email Verification:**
   * **To:** `[Insert Official Submission Email Here]`
   * **Subject:** `Registration Data Backup - Tab [ X ]`
   * **Attachment / Body:** Do not change or edit the attached file or message contents.
5. Tap the **Send** button (the blue arrow in the top right corner).
   * *Note on Connectivity:* If there is no active Wi-Fi, the email will sit securely in the Mail Outbox and will send automatically as soon as the tablet connects to the network.

---

## 5. Return to App & Confirm Sync (Critical Step)
1. Immediately switch back to the registration app.
2. A confirmation prompt will appear on your screen:
   * **"Did you send the email?"**
3. Tap **Yes / Confirm**.
4. The **Sync Pending** counter will reset to zero, indicating that the current batch has been registered as synced.
5. Continue registering attendees.

---

## Quick Rules for Volunteers
* **Always Confirm:** Never skip tapping **Yes / Confirm** when returning from the Mail app, or the pending counter will remain uncleared.
* **Sync Frequently:** Run the sync routine every 30 to 45 minutes or during quiet moments between queues.
* **Stay on the App:** If the tablet locks due to inactivity, simply unlock it and resume right from where you left off.
